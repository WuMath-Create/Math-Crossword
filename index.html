HOW TO PLAY

Complete the daily crossword puzzle using CCSS math vocabulary words!

  • Level 1: 5 words
  • Level 2: 10 words
  • Level 3: 20 words
  • Click a cell to select it. Click again to toggle between Across and Down.
  • Use your keyboard to enter letters.
  • Press Check to evaluate full words—entire correct words turn green, incorrect full words turn red.
